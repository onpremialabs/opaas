What OPAAS does

OPAAS audits AI that is already running on your premises: the models an engine serves, the agents built on them, and the system they form together. It measures how each model performs and behaves, and how auditable each agent is, and turns the result into a signed certificate a third party can verify offline. Every figure is given per category, with its interval; nothing is folded into one score.
